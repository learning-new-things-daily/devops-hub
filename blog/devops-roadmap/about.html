🚀 About DevOps Roadmap Tracker

Learn DevOps with Fun!

This roadmap tracker turns your DevOps learning journey into a game! Mark topics as "Learning" or "Completed" and earn XP for every milestone. Track your progress visually and stay motivated with daily streaks.

Stages & Medals

  • Levels: Gain XP by completing topics. Level up as you learn more!
  • Streaks: Keep your daily learning streak alive for bonus motivation.
  • Medals:
    • 🥉 Bronze: Complete 5 topics
    • 🥈 Silver: Complete 15 topics
    • 🥇 Gold: Complete 30 topics

Backup & Import

Backup: Download your progress as a ZIP file anytime. This keeps your achievements safe.
Import: Restore your progress from a backup ZIP file if you switch devices or need to recover.

Checkpoint Save: Enable auto-backup to save your progress every minute as a checkpoint in your browser. This is great for accidental refreshes, but always use the full backup before major resets!

How to Use

  1. Mark topics as you learn.
  2. Watch your XP bar and streak grow.
  3. Earn medals for your achievements.
  4. Use backup before resetting or switching devices.
  5. Import backup to restore your progress.
